Hostel Room Application Procedure

Students wishing to apply for a hostel room must submit the online hostel application form available on the student portal. Applications open at the start of each semester and close two weeks before the semester begins.

Required documents include a copy of the student ID card, a passport-sized photograph, and proof of local address if applicable. Priority is given to first-year students and students living more than 50 kilometers from campus.

Room allocation results are typically announced within 10 working days of the application deadline. Students who are not allocated a room in the first round may apply again during the second allocation window, which opens two weeks after the first round closes.

For questions about the hostel application process, students should contact the Hostel Administration Office located in the main administration building, room 105.
